02 · Company

Who we are

Indumiso Design Solutions (Pty) Ltd (IDS) was founded in 2017 as a digital design company based in Nkandla, KwaZulu-Natal. Today, construction and the built environment lead the commercial offering, supported by architecture, design and digital visualisation.
